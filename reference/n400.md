# Single-trial N400 amplitudes

30 participants read German sentences ending in an expected or
unexpected word (*Er trinkt seinen Kaffee / Schrank*). For each trial,
the mean EEG amplitude 300-500 ms after the final word at nine
electrodes. The N400 is more negative the less predictable the word
(lower cloze probability), and largest over centro-parietal sites. 80
items, two counterbalanced lists.

## Usage

``` r
n400
```

## Format

A tibble with 21600 rows and 9 columns:

- participant:

  Participant ID, `P01` to `P30`.

- list:

  Counterbalancing list, 1 or 2.

- item:

  Item ID, `i01` to `i80`.

- condition:

  `"expected"` or `"unexpected"`.

- cloze:

  Cloze probability of the final word.

- electrode:

  `"F3"`, `"Fz"`, `"F4"`, `"C3"`, `"Cz"`, `"C4"`, `"P3"`, `"Pz"` or
  `"P4"`.

- anteriority:

  `"frontal"`, `"central"` or `"parietal"`.

- laterality:

  `"left"`, `"midline"` or `"right"`.

- amplitude:

  Mean amplitude in microvolts.

## Source

`data-raw/n400.R`

## Details

True model:
`amplitude = 2 + p0 + i0 + e0 + weight * (-4 + p1) * (1 - cloze) + trial + e`.
The electrode weight is 1 at Pz, 0.85 to 0.9 at other parietal and
central sites and 0.5 to 0.6 at frontal sites; `e0` is a small electrode
offset. By-participant intercepts `p0` (SD 1.5) and N400 slopes `p1` (SD
1); by-item intercepts `i0` (SD 0.5); trial noise shared across
electrodes (SD 4) and electrode noise (SD 3). Cloze is 0.5 to 0.95 for
expected and 0 to 0.05 for unexpected words.

## Examples

``` r
pz <- subset(n400, electrode == "Pz")
aggregate(amplitude ~ condition, data = pz, FUN = mean)
#>    condition amplitude
#> 1   expected  0.589250
#> 2 unexpected -2.103867
if (requireNamespace("lme4", quietly = TRUE)) {
  m <- lme4::lmer(amplitude ~ cloze + (1 + cloze | participant), data = pz)
  lme4::fixef(m)
}
#> (Intercept)       cloze 
#>    -2.15742     3.73584 
round(tapply(n400$amplitude, n400[c("anteriority", "condition")], mean), 2)
#>            condition
#> anteriority expected unexpected
#>    frontal      1.84       0.27
#>    central      0.95      -1.47
#>    parietal     0.47      -1.94
```
